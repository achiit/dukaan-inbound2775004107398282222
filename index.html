About the school

A school built for the children of our own area.

Since 2018 we have run M.B. Public School on one simple belief: a child in Sikarara should not have to travel far to get good teaching. Today 368 children study with us, and their families have given us their trust, and we try to earn it every morning at 8 o'clock.

We know most parents by name, and we know their children too. If a student is struggling, we talk to the parents early, not at result time.

Come, see the classrooms, meet the teachers, and then decide. That is how we like it.

  • Teachers who know every child by name
  • Regular tests and honest feedback to parents
  • Safe, supervised campus during school hours
  • Focus on reading, writing and clear speaking
  • Easy to reach: call or WhatsApp anytime
